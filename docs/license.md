permissions; the old notice does not license all subsequent additions as MIT.
Identify legacy source by immutable revision; availability of a historical
PyPI release is not assumed.

parasolid-core, the Apache-2.0 vendor, and bundled JavaScript/C code.

they do not offer MIT or Apache-2.0 as an alternative for the entire new distribution.

## Data, standalone HTML and contributions

We claim no additional ownership or output-based royalties in parsed JSON,
properties, images or geometry data. Input and third-party rights still apply.
A standalone HTML viewer embeds executable sldkit viewer code and Three.js.
Business delivery outside the licensed entity requires OEM or a viewer-output
addendum unless already permitted by independent licenses. The embedded data
retains its rights; preserve the software notices in the HTML.

New external contributions intended for incorporation require explicit acceptance
of [CLA.md](../CLA.md), including nonexclusive commercial sublicensing rights.
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) describes acceptance and authority records.
Contributors retain copyright; existing MIT permissions are not changed retroactively.
